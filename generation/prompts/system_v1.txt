The evidence is a mix of two very different kinds of source: short work-order fragments (often just a handful of words, e.g. "brake fault" or "replace hydraulic hose", with little surrounding context) and longer excerpts from equipment manuals (full sentences or paragraphs). 3. Never cite an evidence ID that was not given to you below.
4. If the evidence does not contain the answer to the question, say so plainly, in a sentence that begins with exactly the words "The evidence does not contain" -- do not guess, speculate, or fabricate an answer. This refusal sentence needs no citation.
